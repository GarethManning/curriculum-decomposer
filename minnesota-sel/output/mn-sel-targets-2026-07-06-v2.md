- **Canonical form:** every statement is `I can …`, one observable verb, one construct (integrative targets excepted — they deliberately require components in concert). No parentheses, examples, vague comparatives, or third-person subject.
- **Channel:** `explain` (declarative/conceptual — evidenced by explaining) · `do` (performance in situation) · `both`. Replaces Type 1/2/3 routing.
- **Provenance:** every Minnesota-derived target carries its parent `benchmark_id` + exact verbatim (234/234). Integrative targets carry `learnos-integrative` — no MN benchmark, by design.
- **Assessability re-split (Pass 1):** 1 remerge (recognize/label → one target on the observable verb; covert element captured by the support ladder), 33 kept-split. 3 boundary calls ruled single integrated constructs.
- **Modality:** `decided` = source wording preserved (already canonical); `proposed` = rewritten or derived/integrative (clinician confirms). Every strategy_link = `proposed` pending Kari.
